Manchester United Football Club is a professional football club based in Old Trafford, Greater Manchester, England, that competes in the Premier League, the top flight of English football. Nicknamed the Red Devils ,   the club was founded as Newton Heath LYR Football Club in eighteen seventy eight, changed its name to Manchester United in nineteen o two and moved to its current stadium, Old Trafford, in nineteen ten .Manchester United have won more trophies than any other club in English football, with a record twenty League titles, twelve FA Cups, five League Cups and a record twenty one FA Community Shields. United have also won three European Cups/UEFA Champions Leagues, one UEFA Europa League, one UEFA Cup Winners' Cup, one UEFA Super Cup, one Intercontinental Cup and one FIFA Club World Cup. In nineteen ninety nine, the club became the first in the history of English football to achieve the continental European treble.
